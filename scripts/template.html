Factor Screen Dashboard

Five screens, each name scored 0–100 on Growth, Value, Quality, Momentum and Income (ranked within its own screen; 50 = middle). The Swing tab scans the broad US market for RSI(14) recovered above 30 while price sits at/below the lower Bollinger band. The two low-side screens also carry RSI(14) and Bollinger Bands(20,2) — fresh RSI crosses below 30 (into oversold) and back above 30 (recovering), plus where the last price sits in its band (%B) and the band's low / mid / high lines. Re-screened on live Webull data, __ASOF__ (intraday); hit Refresh live for current quotes on the active tab. Transparent reconstruction, not Webull's Quant Rating; not investment advice.

Factors (0–100 rank within the active screen): Growth = EPS momentum. Value = cheapness across P/E, P/B, P/S. Quality = ROE proxy. Momentum = position in the 52-week range. Income = dividend-yield rank. Comp = equal-weight average. RSI = 14-day Wilder RSI from daily closes; shaded red below 30; ↓30 marks a fresh cross down from ≥30 to <30 (entering oversold) and ↑30 a cross back up from <30 to ≥30 (recovering), within the last ~5 sessions. Bollinger Bands(20,2) give the lower / middle / upper price lines (20-day average ±2 standard deviations); %B places the last close in that band (0 = lower line, 50 = middle, 100 = upper, below 0 = under the lower band — the oversold edge). The context column is % from 52-week high on the high screens and % off 52-week low on the low screens.

Source: Webull screens, snapshots & daily bars, deduplicated. $1B+ cap floor on the high/low screens. Fast snapshot method, so Growth and Quality are proxies. RSI computed only for the low-side screens (where <30 is meaningful).
